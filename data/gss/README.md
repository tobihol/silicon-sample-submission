# General Social Survey, 1972–2024 Cumulative File (Release 3a)

## Version

NORC GSS Cumulative Data File 1972–2024, release 7224 R3a (July 2026 posting).
Downloaded 2026-08-13 from NORC's public distribution (no account required):
https://gss.norc.org/us/en/gss/get-the-data.html

Data citation: Davern, Michael; Bautista, Rene; Freese, Jeremy; Herd, Pamela;
and Morgan, Stephen L.; General Social Survey 1972–2024. [Machine-readable
data file]. Principal Investigator, Michael Davern; Co-Principal Investigators,
Rene Bautista, Jeremy Freese, Pamela Herd, and Stephen L. Morgan. NORC ed.
Chicago: NORC, 2025.

- `downloads/GSS_stata/gss7224_r3a.dta` — the full cumulative microdata

- `downloads/GSS_stata/GSS 2024 Codebook R3a.pdf` — the codebook (also at
  `downloads/GSS_2024_Codebook.pdf`)
- `downloads/GSS_stata/Release Notes 7224 R3a.pdf`, `GSS 2024 - Whats New R3a.pdf`,
  `GSS 2024 Release Variables R3a.pdf`, `ReadMe.txt` — release documentation

## Why it is here

The Silicon Sample Benchmark's registration form (item D.1) names the GSS as an
example source for constructing demographic profiles. Beyond profiles, the GSS
carries decades of U.S. items on confidence in institutions and in the
scientific community (e.g. `consci`), environmental spending attitudes
(`natenvir`), and standard demographics — usable as training/validation ground
truth for prediction pipelines. Public, redistributable for research.
